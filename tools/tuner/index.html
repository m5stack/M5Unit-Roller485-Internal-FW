Tuning guide

Mushy / weak click → raise P gain and torque limit; lower dead zone % so the spring engages earlier.
Draggy between detents → lower D gain, raise dead zone %, raise coast threshold (coasts more).
Buzz / oscillation at center → lower P, add a little D (0.5–3), or raise dead zone %.
Double-clicks / chatter at the boundary → raise snap point toward 0.6–0.8 (more hysteresis).
Runs away when spun → lower velocity cutoff; if it runs away slowly, the torque sign is wrong (firmware).
Drifts off center at rest → lower idle rate alpha or raise idle delay.
